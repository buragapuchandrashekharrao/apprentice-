This comprehensive final-week data science project integrates the complete analytical workflow developed across the internship. The project uses the Titanic passenger dataset for data preparation, exploratory visualization, statistical analysis, and hypothesis testing, and incorporates a supervised machine-learning benchmark from Week 4 to demonstrate predictive modeling and evaluation. The report begins with data understanding and cleaning, including missing-value treatment, categorical standardization, outlier handling, encoding, and reproducible preprocessing. It then presents visual storytelling techniques that reveal important differences in survival across sex, passenger class, age, and fare.

The statistical stage formalizes the key finding that passenger sex and survival were strongly associated. Female passengers had an observed survival rate of approximately 74.2%, compared with 18.9% for male passengers, producing a difference of about 55.3 percentage points. Chi-square, Fisher’s exact test, proportion testing, confidence intervals, and effect-size analysis provide complementary evidence while avoiding unsupported causal conclusions.

The machine-learning stage demonstrates a leakage-safe Logistic Regression pipeline with standardized features. On a held-out test set, the model achieved 98.25% accuracy, 98.61% precision, 98.61% recall, 98.61% F1-score, and a ROC-AUC of 0.995, with only two classification errors. The report emphasizes that high benchmark performance does not replace cross-validation, external validation, error-cost analysis, calibration, or responsible governance.

Finally, the project converts analytical evidence into strategic recommendations. These include establishing data-quality gates, making subgroup analysis standard practice, selecting metrics according to decision costs, using cross-validation and independent validation, preserving explainability, and maintaining reproducible reporting pipelines. Business and research impacts, ethical considerations, limitations, and a phased future-improvement roadmap are also documented. The final report therefore demonstrates not only technical competence, but the ability to translate data insights into clear, defensible, and actionable stakeholder recommendations.
